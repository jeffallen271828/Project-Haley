# Haley Voice Actor Studio Recording Sheet

**Neural TTS / Voice Cloning Dataset Session Guide**

**Project:** Haley Conversational Embedded AI System

### Target Use
- Neural voice cloning
- XTTS training/adaptation
- Conversational speech synthesis
- Emotion-conditioned TTS
- Interactive dialogue generation

---

## 1. Recording Environment Requirements

### Room Requirements
- Quiet room with minimal reflections
- No HVAC rumble if possible
- No fans or open windows
- Avoid untreated large rooms
- Soft furnishings preferred

---

## 2. Recording Settings

**Preferred Format**
- WAV
- Mono
- 24-bit
- 48kHz

**Minimum acceptable:**
- 16-bit
- 44.1kHz

---

## 3. Delivery Style Notes

### Haley Personality Notes
Haley is:
- relaxed
- warm
- affectionate
- slightly sleepy
- casually sarcastic
- emotionally attentive
- low-energy but engaged

### Performance Notes

**DO:**
- speak naturally
- leave small natural pauses
- use conversational rhythm
- sound grounded and authentic
- slightly drawl certain phrases
- maintain warmth

**DO NOT:**
- overact
- sound theatrical
- sound announcer-like
- exaggerate anime energy
- rush lines
- over-enunciate unnaturally

---

## 4. File Naming Convention

**Recommended:**  
`haley_[section]_[take#].wav`

**Examples:**
- `haley_neutral_001.wav`
- `haley_emotion_warm_003.wav`
- `haley_dialogue_012.wav`

---

## 5. Session Structure

**Recommended order:**

1. Neutral Coverage
2. Technical / Phonetic Coverage
3. Numbers and Abbreviations
4. Haley Conversational Style
5. Emotional Variations
6. Dialogue Passes
7. Cleanup / Alternate Takes

---

## 6. Recording Instructions

### Take Procedure
For **EVERY** line:

1. Pause 1–2 seconds before speaking
2. Deliver line naturally
3. Pause 1 second afterward
4. Repeat if necessary

**Recommended:** 2–3 takes per line (choose best performance later)

## 13. Final Cleanup Pass

Optional additional recordings:

- sighs
- soft laughs
- sleepy hums
- thoughtful pauses
- breathing transitions
- small conversational acknowledgements

**Examples:** “Mm-hm.” “Yeah.” “Heh.” “Okay.” “Sure.” “Alright.” “Hm.”

---

## 14. Final Export Recommendations

After recording:

1. Remove failed takes
2. Trim excessive silence
3. **Do NOT** heavily noise reduce
4. **Do NOT** normalize aggressively
5. Avoid compression/reverb
6. Preserve natural dynamics

---

## 15. Recommended Dataset Size

- **Minimum Viable:** 15–30 minutes
- **Recommended:** 1–2 hours
- **Excellent:** 3–5+ hours

> More emotional variation generally improves expressive TTS significantly.

---

## 16. Most Important Performance Notes

The most convincing conversational voices are:
- understated
- natural
- emotionally grounded
- conversationally imperfect
- rhythmically believable

**Avoid sounding like:**
- commercials
- anime dubbing
- radio hosts
- exaggerated characters

**The goal is:**  
A believable person casually talking to someone they genuinely care about.
